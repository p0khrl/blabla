About

Hi, I’m Pramod, but you can call me p0khrl. I’m into cybersecurity and networking, mostly the part where you work out how systems talk to each other and then how they break.

I build tools around that. Orbis is a modular VPN engine, Ne0 maps external attack surfaces, and RouteZero models attack paths for authorized security research.

I read a lot of writeups and get stuck on challenges for way longer than I should, and I enjoy it anyway. Away from the keyboard, I’m watching anime movies. I’ve been on a bit of a binge lately.
